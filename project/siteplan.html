Website Planning Document

Site Name

Naija Food Guide

Naija Food Guide is a website dedicated to introducing visitors to Nigerian cuisine. The name combines "Naija," a familiar nickname for Nigeria, with "Food Guide" to clearly communicate the purpose of the website.

Site Purpose

The site will serve as an easy-to-use guide to Nigerian cuisine. Visitors will be able to discover popular Nigerian dishes, learn about their ingredients and preparation methods, and explore information about the cultural significance of Nigerian food.

Scenarios

  • What Nigerian dishes should I try if I am new to Nigerian food?
  • What ingredients are commonly used in Nigerian cooking?
  • How are popular Nigerian dishes prepared?

Color Schema

The color scheme is inspired by Nigerian culture while maintaining a clean and modern appearance.

Deep Green — #006B3C

Used for the header, navigation, headings, and major accents.

Cream — #FFF8E7

Used as the main page background.

Gold — #F4B400

Used for buttons, highlights, borders, and smaller accents.

Dark Charcoal — #222222

Used for body text to provide strong readability.

Typography

Playfair Display

Used for the site name, main headings, and section headings.

Montserrat

Used for body text, navigation, buttons, labels, and supporting information.
